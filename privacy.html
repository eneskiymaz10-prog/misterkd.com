Privacy notice.

Last updated 4 November 2026

misterkd.com is the personal website of Kadir Demir, known as Mister KD. This notice explains what happens to the details you share here. It is short because we collect very little.

Who is responsible

Kadir Demir is responsible for the personal data processed through this website. You can reach him at kadir@biteandmore.com.

What we collect and why

  • Your brief. When you send a brief, we receive your name, your email address, the topic you chose, your message and, if you add it, your company. We use these only to reply to you and, if we work together, to run that project. We rely on your request and on our legitimate interest in answering business enquiries.
  • Your visit. The site is hosted on GitHub Pages. Like any web server, it processes technical data such as your IP address and browser type to deliver the pages and keep them secure. We do not use cookies, advertising trackers or social media pixels.

Who receives it

Your brief reaches Kadir by email, either through your own email app or through Web3Forms, a form delivery service that forwards it to his inbox. We do not sell your details or share them for marketing. Some of these service providers process data outside your country, including in the United States.

How long we keep it

We keep enquiries for as long as we need them to reply and to handle any project that follows. Enquiries that do not lead to a project are deleted within 24 months.

Your rights

You can ask to see, correct or delete the personal data we hold about you, or object to how we use it, by emailing kadir@biteandmore.com. You can also complain to a data protection authority: in Türkiye the Personal Data Protection Authority (KVKK), in the EU the authority in your country.

Changes

If this notice changes, the date at the top changes with it.
