04 — How it gets made

Taste. Test.
Try again.

1,000+ product trials. The work is in the details: ingredients, nutrition, texture and what people come back for.

Work with KD ↗
  1. 01

    Ingredient testing

    Compare taste, source, cost and stability before an ingredient earns its place in the formula.

  2. 02

    Nutrition balancing

    Set the protein, sugar and calorie targets. Reformulate until the taste and texture work with them.

  3. 03

    Texture engineering

    Test the crunch, chew and melt. A nutrition label cannot fix a bite people don’t enjoy.

  4. 04

    Global benchmarking

    Compare flavours, formats and price points against what shoppers already choose in each market.

  5. 05

    Consumer feedback

    Let people taste it. Use their feedback to decide what stays, what changes and what needs another trial.

  6. 06

    Retail performance

    Follow sales, repeat purchase and margin after launch. Use the shelf data to improve the next batch.
