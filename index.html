My process

0+
trials. And counting.

I don't believe in shortcuts. I believe in iteration, precision and obsession. Every product goes through the same cycle until it wins on the shelf — not just in the lab.

  1. 01Ingredient testing

    Source, taste, cost, stability. Hundreds of samples before one moves on.

  2. 02Macro balancing

    Protein up, sugar out, calories capped — without losing the treat.

  3. 03Texture engineering

    Crunch, chew, melt. The part most “healthy” brands skip.

  4. 04Global benchmarking

    Every flavour measured against the best in its category, in its market.

  5. 05Consumer feedback loops

    Real people, real repurchase. If they don't buy twice, back to 01.

  6. 06Retail performance analysis

    Shelf data decides. Velocity, margin, returns.
